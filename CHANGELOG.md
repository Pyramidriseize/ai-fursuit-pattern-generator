# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-27)

First public build.

- Pattern studio: describe the fursona, get printable tiled PDF pieces scaled to your measurements.
- Parametric base bodies built on the FreeSewing engine, with seam lengths matched.
- Cut lists with grain lines and seam allowances.
- Art studio mockups and the species gallery.
- One portable zip: unpack and run, nothing else to install.
